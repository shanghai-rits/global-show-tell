Global Learning,
Artificial Intelligence,
& Interactivity

NYU Global Show & Tell is a festival that celebrates creativity and community through a global showcase of student works that emerge within NYU's global network.

In a world where knowledge once spread through ancient wayfarers, we explore how artificial intelligence mediates global learning and multidisciplinary collaborations through student works that bring together artificial intelligence and interactive media.

NYU Goddard Impact Group Award supported this initiative.
