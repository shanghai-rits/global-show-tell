Connecting minds and communities across NYU Shanghai, NYU New York, and NYU Abu Dhabi, NYU Global Show & Tell is a collaborative platform designed to explore the intersections of artificial intelligence, creativity, and education.

What started as an idea has grown into a lively mix of online showcases, campus galleries, public exhibitions, and cross-campus events, ultimately building strong momentum for creative AI practices and interdisciplinary explorations.

This initiative was made possible through the generous support of the NYU Goddard Impact Group Award.

Meet the Organizers

The project involved faculty, staff, and students across NYU campuses and communities. NYU Shanghai IMA and NYU Shanghai Library served as the primary organizing hub. NYU ITP/IMA activated its local community by hosting an on-site exhibition and lightning talks. Other NYU sites also provided organizational support.

Our Journey

  1. Call for Ideas and Collaboration (January – March 2025)

    We launched our journey with an open call for submissions and collaboration. Professor Jung Hyun Moon and Professor Aidan Nelson led the selection process to curate our online and physical exhibitions.

  2. Launch at NYU Shanghai (April 13, 2025)

    We officially kicked off with a day of celebration at NYU Shanghai designed to inspire students, faculty, and broader audiences. The launch featured an interactive student artist showcase, a cross-disciplinary panel discussion, and a workshop.

  3. Networked Art: Live-Diffusion (April 9–13, 2025)

    Setting the stage for the launch, the opening project Live-Diffusion brought AI artwork to life. Created by student developers Jinran Ye and Chenxuan Wu, this networked, real-time AI-generated installation bridged spaces and activities across NYU New York, NYU Shanghai, and NYU Abu Dhabi simultaneously.

  4. Bridging New York & Shanghai (October 9–26, 2025)

    Our autumn showcase, "Exploring Creativity in the Age of AI," connected NYU ITP/IMA in New York with NYU Shanghai’s IMA. This iteration began at ITP/IMA with an exhibition and lightning talks, followed immediately by an exhibition at the IMA Gallery at NYU Shanghai.

  5. Expanding to the Public Stage (October – December 2025)

    What began as an internal academic exchange quickly broke past campus walls and expanded into broader public spheres. Between October 31 and November 2, 2025, we showcased 19 Global Show & Tell projects at the FutureLab Art and Design Innovation Expo at Shanghai's West Bund Art Center, followed by a spotlight showcase of selected student works at Future Theatre in December 2025. Our exhibition at the FutureLab 2025 drew an estimated 2,500 to 3,000 visitors.

Why It Matters

For our student creators, stepping out of the classroom into public art venues was a valuable learning experience. Direct interaction with public visitors helped students build confidence and see the tangible impact of their ideas. For faculty, stepping into professional exhibition spaces provided a vital pedagogical framework, which allowed students to test interactive work in a professional context and receive feedback from diverse global audiences.
