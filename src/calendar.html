FutureLab Art and Design Innovation Expo

Time:
October 31 - November 2, 2025
Location:
West Bund Art Center, Shanghai
NYU Global Show & Tell, together with the NYU Shanghai IMA Program and the NYU Tisch IMA Masters Program, presented a collection of works at the FutureLab exhibition.

Themed “Currents,” the showcase highlights the many currents and paths traced by each artist as students, graduates, and fellows.

A current indicates a continuous and sustained directional movement, not simply an entropic diffusion of matter, but something focused and resilient. The directionality and attraction are strengthened by the accumulation of elements, of participants, and colliding forces aligning through subtle magnetism, cohering into a stronger whole. Currents shift over time, snaking and twisting, while holding a soft-focused form, a malleable entity with no skin or shell to contain itself. A current is energy, pulled along, driven through attraction, leaving traces and imprints along the way.
